# Imagens da hero (ilha flutuante)

Referência de composição: https://br.pinterest.com/pin/418905202857774331/

Arquivos em uso: `dist/assets/hero/island.webp` e `rock-1.webp`, `rock-2.webp`, `rock-3.webp` (as três pedras são a mesma imagem gerada, girada e espelhada).
As imagens foram geradas no Flow com fundo branco; o fundo foi recortado para transparência e a imagem aparada.
Se um arquivo faltar, a hero mostra um desenho SVG provisório.

## Prompt 1 — ilha (16:9)

Photorealistic floating island, isolated on a pure white seamless background (#FFFFFF). An inverted cone-shaped chunk of earth seen from a slightly elevated front angle: the flat oval top is covered with dry olive-green and golden wild grass tufts around the rim and a bare patch of dark brown gravel soil in the center; the underside is a rough dry-stacked mass of beige, tan and sand-colored angular limestone rocks tapering to a single point at the bottom. Soft, even studio lighting from above, natural colors, sharp detail, high-end product photography look. The island is perfectly centered, fully visible with generous white margin on all sides, nothing cropped. No cast shadow on the background, no ground, no sky, no clouds, no bicycle, no people, no text, no logo.

## Prompt 2 — pedras soltas (1:1, gerar 3 variações)

Photorealistic single small rock floating in mid-air, isolated on a pure white seamless background (#FFFFFF). Rough angular piece of beige and tan limestone with subtle brown veins and a few specks of dry moss, irregular natural shape. Soft, even studio lighting from above, sharp detail, product photography look. The rock is centered, fully visible with wide white margin around it. No cast shadow, no ground, no other objects, no text.
